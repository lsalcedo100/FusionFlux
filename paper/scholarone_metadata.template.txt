  This is a decision rather than a default, so the reasoning is recorded here.
  Double-anonymous is IOP's default across the portfolio and single-anonymous
  is the opt-out. The argument for opting out was that this paper's
  credibility rests on the analysis being checkable, and that naming the
  repository is how a referee checks it. That argument is aimed at the editor,
  who reads the cover letter under either model, and the cover letter carries
  the repository URL and the version DOI explicitly for that reason. What the
  referee loses is the link; what the referee also loses is the unaffiliated
  author line that the objection this paper is most exposed to attaches to.
  For a single author with no institution the second is worth more than the
  first.

  The named build under ./ is kept and stays current, both because IOP states
  it will not reject a submission for including author information and because
  the decision is worth being able to reverse at the upload form.
  tools/make_submission.py writes both.
